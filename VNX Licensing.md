<h1 align="center">Venxuix Dccide Licensing</h1>

<p align="center">Venxuix carries a split architecture licensing model.</p>

<p align="center">It is not one program under one license. It is a stack of layers, and a layer carries its own terms wherever it goes. Terms do not merge, and they do not transfer between layers. What governs one part of the system governs that part only.</p>

<p align="center">The licensing across the system is drawn from four:</p>

<div align="center">
<table>
<tr><td align="center"><b>Proprietary</b></td></tr>
<tr><td align="center"><b>LGPL-3.0</b></td></tr>
<tr><td align="center"><b>GPL-3.0</b></td></tr>
<tr><td align="center"><b>Apache-2.0</b></td></tr>
</table>
</div>

<p align="center">Each layer sits under one of them. Some are chosen and some are carried, and a layer's placement is a property of that layer rather than of the system around it. A single distribution can therefore satisfy several of these at once without any of them reaching past its own boundary.</p>
